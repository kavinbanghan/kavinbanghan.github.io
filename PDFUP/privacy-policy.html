Privacy Policy for PdfUp

Effective Date: July 9, 2026

Thank you for using PdfUp. This Privacy Policy explains how PdfUp ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the application.

Information We Collect

PdfUp is designed to process PDF documents primarily on your device.

The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit your personal documents or personal information to our servers.

However, the App uses third-party services that may collect certain information automatically.

Advertising

PdfUp uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may collect information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • App interactions
  • Diagnostic information
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Advertising performance data

This information is collected and processed by Google in accordance with its own privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Learn more about how Google uses data:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

PDF Processing

All PDF operations, including viewing, merging, compressing, splitting, converting, and editing documents, are performed locally on your device unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Your PDF files are not uploaded to our servers.

Permissions

Depending on your Android version, the App may request permissions such as:

  • Access to files and media for opening and saving PDF documents.
  • Notification permission (if applicable).
  • Internet access for displaying advertisements.

These permissions are used only to provide the App's intended functionality.

Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Since PDF processing occurs locally, your documents remain on your device unless you choose to share them using Android's built-in sharing features.

Children's Privacy

PdfUp is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with the updated effective date.
