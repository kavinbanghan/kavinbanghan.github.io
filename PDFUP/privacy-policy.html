This Privacy Policy explains how PdfUp ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the application. ## Information We Collect PdfUp is designed to process PDF documents primarily on your device. The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit your personal documents or personal information to our servers. However, the App uses third party services that may collect certain information automatically. ### Advertising PdfUp uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect information such as: * Advertising ID * Device information * IP address * App interactions * Diagnostic information * Approximate location (derived from IP address) * Advertising performance data This information is collected and processed by Google in accordance with its own privacy policies. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy Learn more about how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads ## PDF Processing All PDF operations, including viewing, merging, compressing, splitting, converting, and editing documents, are performed locally on your device unless explicitly stated otherwise.
