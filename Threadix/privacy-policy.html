Privacy Policy for Threadix

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

Thank you for using Threadix. This Privacy Policy explains how Threadix ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the application.

Threadix is designed to view, render, simulate, and work with supported embroidery and design files on your Android device.

In short: Your design files are processed locally on your device. Threadix does not upload your design files to our servers as part of the normal operation of the App.

1. Information We Collect

Threadix is designed to minimize the collection of personal information. The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information in order to use its core design viewing and rendering functionality.

Your design files, including supported embroidery, CAD, and other supported file formats, are processed on your device for features such as viewing, rendering, simulation, color visualization, and other available design operations.

Threadix does not upload or store your design files on servers operated by us during normal use of the App.

However, third-party services integrated into the App may automatically process certain technical or advertising-related information as described below.

2. Design and File Processing

Threadix may allow you to open, view, render, simulate, inspect, modify, or otherwise work with supported design files stored on your device.

These operations are performed locally on your device unless a feature explicitly states otherwise.

Threadix does not send your design files, embroidery files, CAD files, or their contents to our servers for processing.

Files remain under your control and are accessed only when you choose to open or use them through the App.

3. Advertising

Threadix may use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

When advertisements are displayed, Google and its advertising partners may automatically process certain information associated with the device and use of the App.

Depending on the advertising configuration and applicable privacy requirements, this information may include:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • App and advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic information
  • Approximate location derived from network information
  • Advertising performance information

We do not use Threadix to collect your design files for advertising purposes.

Google's processing of information through its advertising services is governed by Google's own privacy policies and applicable terms.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where required, advertising consent and privacy choices may be presented through Google's applicable consent mechanisms.

4. Firebase Remote Config

Threadix may use Firebase Remote Config to retrieve configuration information from Firebase.

This service may be used to manage application configuration, feature availability, update-related information, or other settings without requiring a new version of the application for every configuration change.

Firebase Remote Config may automatically process certain technical information associated with the application instance, which may include:

  • Country code
  • Language code
  • Time zone
  • Android operating system version
  • Platform version
  • Application package information
  • Firebase application identifiers
  • Firebase SDK version
  • Firebase installation identifiers

Threadix does not use Firebase Remote Config to upload or store your design files.

Firebase services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

Firebase Privacy and Security:
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. Permissions

Depending on your Android version and the features you use, Threadix may request or use permissions or system capabilities such as:

  • File access: To allow you to select and open supported design files and save or export files where applicable.
  • Notifications: If enabled by the App, notifications may be used to inform you about relevant application events or updates.
  • Internet access: To communicate with third-party services such as advertising services and Firebase services.

Threadix uses these permissions and system capabilities only for the functionality for which they are required.

6. Sharing and Exporting Files

Threadix may provide features that allow you to export or share files using Android's built-in file and sharing functionality.

When you choose to share or export a file, the file may be transferred to the application, service, or person that you select.

Such transfers are controlled by you and may be subject to the privacy policies and terms of the third-party application or service you choose to use.

7. Third-Party Services

Threadix may use third-party services to provide specific functionality. These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Third-party services currently used or intended to be used by Threadix may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Remote Config

The specific information processed by these services depends on how the respective services are configured and used within Threadix.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Threadix.

Because Threadix processes design files locally on your device, your design files are not transmitted to our servers as part of normal application operation.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining appropriate security of your device and any files stored on it.

9. Data Retention

Threadix does not maintain a server-side copy of your design files as part of its normal file viewing and rendering functionality.

Files saved on your device remain subject to the storage and file management settings of your Android device.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob or Firebase is retained and managed according to the respective service provider's policies and configurations.

10. Children's Privacy

Threadix is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

11. Your Choices

You control which files you open, process, export, or share using Threadix.

You may also manage applicable Android permissions through your device settings.

Advertising and privacy choices may be available through the consent and privacy controls provided by Google and Android, depending on your location and applicable requirements.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Threadix, its features, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements.

Any changes will be posted on this page along with an updated effective date.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how Threadix handles information.
